# Market — One Page

## Competitor table

| Player | What they do | Why they're not MediSimple |
|---|---|---|
| **OpenEvidence** | Clinician AI search/answers | Sells to doctors & health systems, not families |
| **Abridge** | AI clinical documentation | Ambient scribe for providers; patient is not the user |
| **Commure** | Hospital workflow AI | Enterprise provider tooling |
| **Navina** | AI for value-based care ops | Clinician/insurer side |
| **Luffu** (ex-Fitbit founders) | Aging-in-place hardware + caregiver monitoring | Closest overlap — but hardware-first, US-household-first |
| Generic PHR apps | Personal health records | Dumps of PDFs; no plain-language understanding, no family graph |

## MediSimple's wedge

1. **History-first, not device-first.** The defensible asset is the ingested history and the personal baseline it produces — competitors sell encounters; MediSimple accumulates a person.
2. **Cross-border family as the primary user.** ~40M+ US immigrants are the health decision-makers for a parent abroad — different language, pharmacy, phone format, no shared doctor. Nobody builds for this household shape.
3. **Plain-language as the interface.** The report explains itself in the patient's own language. Competitors assume clinical literacy.

## Why now

- **Claude-quality summarization is cheap enough** to run per-report at consumer scale — this product was economically impossible five years ago.
- **Diaspora care coordination is unfunded and unowned:** wearables assume co-located families; telehealth assumes a shared system. The gap between them is where MediSimple lives.
- **Aging demographics on both sides of the border** — the 58-year-old with diabetes and a heart condition is the fastest-growing patient profile in India and the US.

## Honest caveats

- No moat exists until the history exists: the flywheel (docs/moat.md) only spins with real usage.
- Regulatory path (FDA general-wellness vs. clinical claims) is deliberately deferred — see `docs/security.md` roadmap. Wellness language only, everywhere, until then.
